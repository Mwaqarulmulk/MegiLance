# MegiLance Blog SEO Implementation Notes

## Site topic clusters

Use the blog to build topical authority around these clusters:

1. AI freelancing platform
2. Hire freelancers online
3. Find freelance jobs online
4. Freelance escrow and payment protection
5. Smart contract escrow for freelancing
6. Freelance pricing and proposal writing
7. Pakistani and emerging-market freelancing
8. AI tools for freelancers and clients
9. Transparent freelance marketplace
10. Web2/Web3 freelance marketplace

## Internal linking plan

Every article should link to at least 2-4 relevant MegiLance pages:

- Home: https://megilance.site/
- How it works: https://megilance.site/how-it-works
- Clients: https://megilance.site/clients
- Freelancers: https://megilance.site/freelancers
- AI Hub: https://megilance.site/ai
- Pricing: https://megilance.site/pricing
- About: https://megilance.site/about
- FAQ: https://megilance.site/faq

## On-page SEO checklist

- One H1 only.
- Use focus keyword in title, first paragraph, one H2, meta description, slug, and image alt text.
- Use natural secondary keywords in subheadings and body.
- Keep slugs short and descriptive.
- Add FAQ section near the end.
- Add BlogPosting JSON-LD from `schema_jsonld/`.
- Add at least one custom image per article.
- Link out to trusted sources when making factual claims outside MegiLance.
- Update each article every 3-6 months.
